# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

## Stack
Unity 6, URP, C# 9. iPad first, then iPhone and Mac.

## Users
Players exploring a fantasy village through touch controls in landscape.

## Product Purpose
Living World is a third-person fantasy life RPG. Success means discovering that yesterday's action changed the village through simulation rules.

## Operating Context
The first Unity surface is an Apple shop and village slice, confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-04.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Simulation owns facts. UI sends commands and reads snapshots. NPC knowledge is separate from world truth. Approved content is in Content/. No weather or health system may be invented for presentation.

## Brand Commitments
Approved Kenney low-poly art, immersive minimal HUD, large touch targets, iPad landscape safe areas.

## Evidence on Hand
README.md, approved docs/design/, Content/, simulation tests and Apple Test logs. Asset licenses are recorded in Assets/README.md.

## Product Principles
- Systems produce consequences.
- Display only information the player can discover.
- Keep the village visible while interacting.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Touch targets at least 44 by 44 points, safe-area layout, legible contrast.
