# P1-07: Shops, wallets and atomic purchases

**Role:** Economy
**Branch:** task/P1-07-shop-trade
**Depends on:** P1-03, P1-06 (merged)

## Goal
Provide deterministic shop accounts and full, partial or failed purchases that
move stock and integer copper atomically and append world-truth events.

## Context
Read AGENTS.md and its required startup documents, then ARCHITECTURE sections 4
and 9, docs/design/ECONOMY.md, and approved economy/item JSON. This task builds
general trade rules only; it does not script Apple Test demand or restocking.

- SimulationTests/Economy/

## You must not edit
- Anything outside the folders above. No Content, docs, configuration, Unity,
  dependencies, git writes or child agents.

## Requirements
1. C# 9, .NET Standard 2.1 compatible, tests first, no Unity/dependencies.
2. Add a nonnegative integer-copper wallet. Debit/credit/transfer operations use
   checked arithmetic, reject nonpositive amounts and are atomic on insufficient
   funds or destination overflow. Self-transfer is a validated no-op. Money is
   not an item.
3. A shop has a unique valid location, NPC owner, stock Inventory, owner wallet,
   and immutable/deterministically ordered positive integer unit prices. Reject
   duplicate/unknown items and invalid values. State remains caller-owned and
   accessible for later persistence; no static/global registry.
4. Purchase input identifies buyer ActorId, buyer Inventory/wallet, item,
   positive requested quantity and whether partial fulfillment is accepted.
   Actual quantity is the requested amount only when stock and funds allow; with
   partial allowed it is the minimum of request, stock and affordable quantity;
   otherwise zero. Do not silently grant credit or clamp malformed input.
5. Preflight all validation, destination capacity and seller-wallet overflow
   before mutation. A successful transaction moves actual stock and exactly
   `actual * unitPrice` copper with checked arithmetic. Any failure leaves stock
   and both wallets unchanged; normal trading conserves total copper and items.
6. Append exactly one event at WorldState.Clock and the shop location after a
   committed outcome: Purchase for full, PartialPurchase for positive partial,
   FailedPurchase for zero. Actor is buyer; target is shop owner; item is set;
   quantity/copper are actual transferred amounts (zero allowed on failure).
   Event logging failure must not leave a partially committed transaction: use
   validation/preflight so the existing log append cannot fail after mutation,
   or append before mutation only when subsequent operations are proven no-fail.
7. Return an immutable result exposing outcome, requested/actual quantity and
   paid copper. No pricing response, restock, theft, content loader or commands.
8. Keep the change near 500 lines including tests; propose a split if needed.

## Tests required
- Full purchase transfers exact stock/copper and logs Purchase.
- Wants 7 with 2 available and partial accepted buys 2/logs PartialPurchase;
  partial declined buys 0/logs FailedPurchase.
- Insufficient funds partial/fail, unknown item, invalid quantity, arithmetic
  overflow and incompatible destination are atomic.
- Normal trade conserves combined item and copper totals; self-transfer safety.
- Same inputs produce identical results/events; read-only deterministic prices.
- Full Debug/Release suites and `git diff --check`.
