**Role:** Economy
**Branch:** task/P1-16c-restocking

## Goal
Create saveable low-stock orders and fulfill them through atomic item/copper transfers.

## Requirements
1. Use immutable caller configuration and caller-owned, restorable order/trigger state.
2. Stock below an explicit threshold creates at most one pending order and records `RestockOrdered` truth.
3. Fulfillment atomically transfers configured stock and exact integer wholesale copper, then records `Restocked` truth.
4. Full-only and explicitly partial fulfillment must remain atomic and conserve items/copper.
5. Shop stock never resets; recreation from state cannot duplicate orders or delivery.
6. Stable deterministic order, C# 9, and no Unity references.

## Tests required
- Stock below 10 creates one order; adequate stock creates none.
- Delivery of 45 at 1 copper conserves all totals.
- Insufficient stock/copper obeys full-or-partial policy atomically.
- Recreated systems do not duplicate pending/completed work.
- Production runs before restocking regardless of registration order.
