# P2-08: Money sources (merchants, travelers, bounties, wages)

**Status:** Completed

**Role:** Economy
**Branch:** task/P2-08-money-in
**Depends on:** P2-07

## Goal
Money enters the village through its named gates only — traveling merchants,
seasonal travelers, wolf bounties — and the internal wage economy (guard's
wage, council stipend, harvest/day labor) redistributes it through the village
fund. Every copper is tracked.

## Context
ECONOMY.md §6–7. Gates for inflow:
1. **Traveling merchants**: every ~3 weeks in warm months, ~800 copper per
   visit — buy apples, timber, honey, smoked meat, Ralf's pelts; sell imports
   (iron at ~10/kg — P2-07 left Doran's exhaustion `RestockOrdered` event
   waiting, with `SmithyState.ResetIronExhaustion()` ready; Tilda's order
   list: salt, cloth, lamp oil, dyes).
2. **Travelers**: seasonal spend at tavern and stores — ~900/month in summer
   and harvest, ~500 in spring/autumn, **almost none in winter** (high ford).
   Avg ~750/month over warm months.
3. **Wolf bounties**: 50 copper/pelt in winter, 2–4 incidents per winter —
   the village's winter windfall (Ralf hunts on retainer).

Wages (redistribution, not new money):
- Bram's guard wage: **20 copper/day from the village fund**.
- Elswith's council stipend: **8/day**.
- Ralf's winter retainer: **60/week in winter** from the fund.
- Harvest hands: **8/day in autumn** (Corvin pays).
- Day labor (the player's market): farm help 8, woodcutting help 6, mill
  help 5, tavern pot-wash 6 + a meal, errands 2–5.
- The fund is fed by a **household levy of 2 copper/week** from the 20, and
  the ~100 background villagers are abstracted as paying ~800/month, so the
  fund balances at ~960/month against Bram (600) + Elswith (240) + Ralf's
  winter retainer + incidentals.

Game starts early autumn — harvest labor and autumn merchant visits matter
immediately.

## Requirements
1. **Merchant visit system**: deterministic schedule (seeded RNG, ~3-week
   cadence in warm months, autumn visit always includes Tilda's order list).
   Each visit: buys village goods up to ~800 copper (apples, timber, honey,
   smoked meat, pelts — take from real stocks, pay real copper into the right
   tills), sells imports (fulfill Doran's iron order first — wire to P2-07's
   exhaustion state; then Tilda's imports at the approved prices). All as
   events; money conserved.
2. **Traveler spend**: a seasonal schedule that pays real copper into Bessa's
   till and the store tills at the design-doc rates (summer/harvest ~900/mo,
   spring/autumn ~500, winter ~0). Deterministic; emits events.
3. **Wolf bounties**: winter only, 2–4 incidents per winter (seeded RNG),
   50 copper each to the pelt-bringer. Events logged.
4. **Village fund**: holds the levy income + abstracted background payments;
   pays Bram 20/day, Elswith 8/day, Ralf's 60/week in winter. Fund state is
   WorldState-owned with internal capture/restore (P2-12 pattern). The fund
   must stay solvent in normal play — prove it in a month-long test.
5. **Harvest/day-labor wages**: NPCs can be paid the approved day rates; at
   minimum model Corvin paying harvest hands 8/day in autumn and the fund
   paying Bram/Elswith/Ralf. Keep a clean `PayWage`/`ChargeLevy` API the
   Agents layer can use later.
