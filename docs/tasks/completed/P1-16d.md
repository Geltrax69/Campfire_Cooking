# P1-16d: Bounded supply-and-demand prices

**Role:** Economy
**Branch:** task/P1-16d-prices
**Depends on:** P1-07, P1-16a

## Goal
Adjust shop unit prices at configured intervals from missed sales and stock bands,
bounded by explicit caller values and recorded as world truth.

## Context
The Apple Test requires sold-out demand to lift the 3-copper apple price to 4,
then settle as supply normalizes. The mechanism must be general, not apple-scripted.

## You may edit
- `Packages/com.geltrax.livingworld.simulation/Runtime/Economy/`
- `SimulationTests/Economy/`

## You must not edit
- Anything outside the folders above. Use `PriceChanged` from P1-16a.

## Requirements
1. Use immutable configurations with explicit item, interval, low/high stock thresholds,
   integer step, minimum and maximum prices; no hidden percentage/rounding defaults.
2. Caller-owned, restorable price state must track processed truth and completed intervals.
3. A failed or partial purchase since the prior adjustment, or low stock, raises price one bounded step.
4. High stock with no missed sale lowers it one bounded step; otherwise price is unchanged.
5. Adjust at most once per configured interval; stable ordinal processing; recreation cannot replay changes.
6. Update the Shop price atomically and append `PriceChanged` with the new unit price in copper.
7. Ignore purchases for other locations/items and never infer demand from NPC hidden state.

## Tests required
- Missed sale raises 3 to 4 but never past configured maximum.
- Low stock raises; surplus without missed sales lowers but never below minimum.
- Normal stock/no missed demand leaves price/event log unchanged.
- Unrelated events do not affect price.
- Recreating from state does not repeat an interval.
