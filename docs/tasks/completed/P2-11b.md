# P2-11b: Phase 2 acceptance — month-long village simulation

**Status:** Completed

**Role:** Test & Scenario
**Branch:** task/P2-11b-acceptance
**Depends on:** P2-11a

## Goal
Prove Phase 2 works as a living village: run the full assembled village for
30 days and assert the economy stays in its band, the bakery sells out, and
relationships measurably move — all emergent, nothing scripted.

## Context
P2-11a built the village assembly and the daily-life drivers (eat, shop,
tavern meetings). This task runs the acceptance simulation the roadmap
promised: "Total village copper stays within ±10% month-to-month; bakery
sells out most days; relationships shift measurably."

## Requirements
1. **Month simulation**: `VillageAssembly.Build`, 30 full days (43,200
   ticks) on a fixed seed. Must complete without exceptions. Deterministic:
   same seed → byte-identical event log (or documented digest equality).
2. **Money band**: total village copper (wallets + tills + funds + pots,
   deduped — follow `ProsperityIndex`'s convention) stays within ±10% of
   the world-open baseline at every month boundary. This is the headline
   assertion — if it fails, tune sinks/sources within their documented
   calibration ranges (do not invent new gates).
3. **Bakery sells out most days**: the bakery's bread stock hits 0 on a
   majority of the 30 days (design: "sells out by early afternoon most
   days"). Assert sellout days > 15.
4. **Food supply tuning** (known gap): the bakery makes ~40 loaves/day but
   the village needs ~96 hunger-points × 20 NPCs ≈ 64 loaves/day at 30
   points/loaf. Close the gap with a documented, design-faithful rule —
   e.g. farm households draw home-grown food (Maren's eggs, farm produce),
   Bessa's tavern sells meals, or the general store stocks food. Pick the
   smallest change that keeps NPCs fed; document the choice and the numbers.
5. **Relationships move**: assert that at least N pairs (document N, e.g.
   10) moved ≥5 trust/affection points from baseline over the month, and
   that at least one rumor spread beyond its origin pair (tavern meetings
   work at village scale).
6. **Report**: write a human-readable month summary with the existing
   `SimulationLogWriter` (or equivalent): opening/closing copper, sellout
   days, biggest relationship movers, notable events.

## Results (seed 42, 30 days)
- Money: 9250 -> 9908 copper (+7.1%, within the ±10% band)
- Bakery sold out 27/30 days
- 52 relationship pairs moved ≥5 trust; strongest new bonds are daily NPC↔Oda trade pairs at 73–81 trust
- StockMissing belief held by 16/20 NPCs; 1 tavern rumor spread (Jory→Piotr)
- Determinism: two 30-day runs, identical SHA256 event-log digests
- Food-gap tuning: NeedsSystem sets IsSleeping from schedule (hunger was accruing 24h/day vs 16h assumed); shopping changed to 2 rye + 2 barley daily; new FarmMealSystem gives the farm household -50 hunger at noon (home-grown food, no copper).
