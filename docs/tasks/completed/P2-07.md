# P2-07: Blacksmith (iron stock, tools, repairs)

**Status:** Completed

**Role:** Economy
**Branch:** task/P2-07-blacksmith
**Depends on:** P2-05

## Goal
Doran Kettle's smithy runs as a real business: a finite iron stock, tool and
nail production, horseshoes, and paid repairs — iron is imported and precious,
so every kilogram is tracked.

## Context
ECONOMY.md: "Iron: merchants → smithy → village. Iron is imported — Doran
cannot make it, only shape it. He keeps ~20 kg stock, buys from traveling
merchants at ~10 copper/kg, sells nails at 1 copper each, tools at 40–80,
horseshoes at 12/set, repairs at 8–25. Every nail is precious because the next
merchant is weeks away." Doran starts with 900 copper and 20 `item_iron_stock`
at `loc_blacksmith` (Content). Iron restock via merchants comes in P2-08;
here the stock is finite and sells down.

## Requirements
1. A world-build step stocks the smithy: 20 kg iron, Doran's till 900 copper,
   following the `GeneralStoreSetup`/`BakeryChainSetup` pattern. Register the
   smithy as a `Shop` (or production point — your choice, document it) at
   `loc_blacksmith`.
2. Production rules (configuration + `WorldState`-owned progress, following
   the P1-16/P2-06 pattern):
   - **Nails**: 1 kg iron → 20 nails (`item_nails`), sold at 1 copper each
     through the existing shop flow (P2-05 already stocks nails at the
     general store — decide: does Tilda buy from Doran wholesale, or do they
     compete? Document the choice; simplest honest rule: Tilda's nail stock
     depletes first, then villagers buy from the smithy — or Doran supplies
     Tilda. Pick one, keep it simple, note it).
   - **Tools**: 2 kg iron → 1 `item_tool_basic`, priced 40–80 by quality
     (document the pricing rule; keep it simple, e.g. fixed 60).
   - **Horseshoes**: 1 kg → 1 set at 12 copper.
   - **Repairs**: a repair *service* — NPC pays 8–25 copper (by item value —
     document the rule), no iron consumed for small repairs, 1 kg for major
     ones; emits a world event.
3. Finite iron: when the 20 kg runs out, production stops and waits for
   merchants (P2-08). Log it as an event.
4. Money conserved everywhere; every production consumes the exact iron.
