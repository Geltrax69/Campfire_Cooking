# P2-06: Bakery chain (grain → mill → flour → bread)

**Status:** Completed

**Role:** Economy
**Branch:** task/P2-06-bakery
**Depends on:** P2-05

## Goal
Crust & Crumb Bakery runs a real production chain: Corvin's grain → Garrick's
mill (1/12 toll in kind) → flour → Oda bakes ~40 loaves each morning and sells
out by early afternoon most days.

## Context
ECONOMY.md §5: autumn harvest ~60 sacks wheat + ~40 barley from Alder Farm;
Corvin carts grain to the mill; Garrick takes the **miller's toll of 1/12 in
kind** and grinds the rest; Oda buys flour at **10 copper/sack**, ~2.5
sacks/day, bakes ~40 loaves/day (one oven, morning bake only). Items exist:
`item_grain`, `item_flour`, `item_bread_rye`, `item_bread_barley`.

## Requirements
1. Model the chain as general production rules (follow the
   `ProductionSystem`/`RestockSystem` pattern from P1-16 — configuration +
   progress state owned by `WorldState`):
   - **Milling**: grain → flour at the mill; Garrick keeps 1/12 of the grain
     as toll (in kind — it becomes his flour/grain stock, not copper).
   - **Baking**: each morning (~05:00–06:00) Oda converts flour → ~40 loaves
     (rye/barley mix — document the split); requires flour on hand, else a
     short bake or none, logged as an event.
   - **Selling**: loaves sell through the existing `Shop` flow at the bakery
     (`loc_bakery`); the oven constraint means no intraday restock.
2. Stock the bakery and mill with sensible starting values (document each:
   granary ~50 sacks reserve per the design doc, Oda's flour on hand, etc.).
3. Bread is food: villagers/NPCs can buy and eat it through existing flows —
   do not build new decision logic (P2-03 territory); scenario/tests drive it.
4. Money conserved: every copper tracked (Oda pays 10/sack flour; toll is in
   kind, not coin).

## Tests required
- A full chain day: grain milled (toll kept in kind), flour bought, 40 loaves
  baked, loaves sell down through the shop.
- No flour → reduced/no bake, logged.
- Oven constraint: no second bake intraday even if flour remains.
- Money conserved across the chain.
- Save → load keeps mill/bakery stocks and production progress.
- Full suite green, Debug + Release.
