# P2-10: Debts (Tilda's tab ledger as a mechanical system)

**Status:** Completed

**Role:** Economy
**Branch:** task/P2-10-debts
**Depends on:** P2-05

## Goal
Tilda's secret ledger is a mechanical debt system: real repayment
schedules, missed payments that break trust, overdue consequences enforced
by reputation, and the Harvest Feast's symbolic forgiveness.

## Context
ECONOMY.md §8. Outstanding tabs (total 340 copper, **no interest**):

| Debtor | Owes | For | Terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Doran Kettle | 120 | Iron stock, bought on credit before the autumn merchant visit | Repays 10/week |
| Bessa Marlowe | 80 | Salt and lamp oil over the summer | Repays ~5/week, irregularly |
| Garrick Alder | 60 | Nails and pitch for wheel patching | Behind; Tilda is patient (Tansy's sake) |
| Jory Reed | 45 | Rope and tar | Repays in smoked fish, 2 fish/week (~8 value) |
| Tam Oakes | 35 | Salt pork last winter | Repays 3/week |

Rules: debts expected within the season; at the **Harvest Feast, debts under
20 are forgiven** (symbolic); after **~60 days overdue**, Tilda tells
Elswith, the council witnesses the debt, the debtor's standing drops — no
one extends them credit, Tilda refuses further tabs, and Bessa's tavern hears
about it (the reputation system does the enforcing). P2-02 defined the
`DebtMissed` event (actor=debtor, targets=[creditor]) and its trust rule —
**this task is its first emitter**.

## Requirements
1. **Debt ledger**: the 5 starting debts with their terms, WorldState-owned
   with internal capture/restore (P2-12 pattern). Weekly repayment ticks
   move copper (or Jory's 2 fish/week) from debtor to Tilda; irregular
   payers (Bessa) use the seeded RNG — document the rule.
2. **Missed repayments emit `DebtMissed`** with actor=debtor,
   targets=[npc_tilda_bray] — this plugs into the P2-02 trust rule, so a
   test must show trust dropping after a missed payment (integration with
   the merged dynamics system).
3. **New tabs**: Tilda extends short credit when a buyer can't pay, gated
   by her trust in the buyer (document the rule); Doran extends short
   credit to people he trusts (same gating, his own values). Keep both
   minimal and rule-driven.
4. **Overdue consequences** (~60 days): the debtor's standing drops via the
   reputation system (P1-18), Tilda refuses further tabs until the old one
   clears, and a rumor reaches the tavern (use the Knowledge rumor path —
   read-only use from Economy is fine; do not move rumor code).
5. **Feast forgiveness**: on Harvest Feast day (find the date in
   docs/design/WORLD.md), debts under 20 are forgiven with an event.
