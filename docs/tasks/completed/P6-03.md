# P6-03: News travel between villages

**Role:** Knowledge agent
**Branch:** task/P6-03-news-travel
**Depends on:** P6-01

## Goal
Implement news and reputation spreading between villages via travelers and merchants (delayed, distorted with distance).

## Context
Phase 6 is "Expanded world". P6-01 built village LOD. This task adds the information connections: what happens in Millbrook eventually reaches King's Rest, but late and distorted.

- Packages/com.geltrax.livingworld.simulation/Runtime/Knowledge/ (inter-village news)
- SimulationTests/Knowledge/ (new test files)

## You must not edit
- Anything outside the folders above. Content/ is approved.

## Requirements
1. **VillageNews**: A news item about a village.
   - NewsId, OriginVillage, AboutVillage (usually same as origin), Content (e.g., "wolf attack", "good harvest", "newcomers arrived"), DayCreated, Severity (0-100)
   - Example: "Millbrook had a wolf attack on day 95, severity 60"
2. **NewsInTransit**: News traveling between villages.
   - News, FromVillage, ToVillage, ArrivalDay (DayCreated + travel time)
   - Travel time = route travel days (from P6-02, or a simple distance-based formula if routes not yet available).
3. **NewsSystem** (IWorldSystem):
   - When a significant event happens in Millbrook (wolf attack, festival, food shortage, etc.), create a VillageNews.
   - Put it in transit to neighboring villages (arrival in 2-5 days depending on distance).
   - When it arrives: the destination village's Mood is affected (good news +, bad news -), and the news is recorded as truth.
   - News can be distorted: with 20% chance (seeded RNG), the severity is ±20 (rumors exaggerate or minimize).
4. **Reputation spread**: Millbrook's outward reputation (from P5-01 TownStats) affects how other villages see it.
   - When news arrives, the destination's opinion of Millbrook shifts based on the news severity and Millbrook's reputation.
   - For now: just track a simple "opinion" value per village pair; document as simplification.
5. **Deterministic**: All distortion and timing uses seeded RNG.

## Tests required
- `NewsTravelsBetweenVillages`: Create news in Millbrook; verify it arrives in King's Rest after travel time.
- `NewsIsDistorted`: Run many trials; verify ~20% of news has distorted severity.
- `BadNewsLowersMood`: Wolf attack news arrives; destination mood decreases.
- `NewsTakesTime`: News created day 1, travel time 3 days; not available at destination on day 2, available on day 4.
