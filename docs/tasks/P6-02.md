# P6-02: Trade routes

**Role:** Economy agent
**Branch:** task/P6-02-trade-routes
**Depends on:** P6-01

## Goal
Implement trade routes between villages: goods flow along routes, prices differ by village, merchants buy low and sell high.

## Context
Phase 6 is "Expanded world". P6-01 built village LOD. This task adds the economic connections: trade routes carrying goods between villages.

## You must not edit

## Requirements
1. **TradeRoute**: Connects two villages.
   - RouteId, FromVillage, ToVillage, TravelDays (int), Goods (list of ItemTypeId that flow)
   - Example: Millbrook → King's Rest, 2 days, goods: apples, cloth
2. **TradeRouteRegistry**: Collection of routes.
   - `Get(routeId)`, `GetFrom(villageId)`, `GetTo(villageId)`
3. **Price differences**: Each village has its own prices for goods.
   - Abstract villages have simple price lists (e.g., King's Rest pays 5 for apples, Millbrook sells for 3).
   - For now: hardcode price differences in the route or village state; document as simplification.
4. **Merchant trade**: A merchant (abstract, not a full NPC) travels a route:
   - Buys goods at the origin village (at origin price).
   - Travels for TravelDays.
   - Sells goods at the destination village (at destination price).
   - Profit = (sell price - buy price) × quantity - travel cost.
   - Record as truth events (MerchantDeparted, MerchantArrived).
5. **Goods flow**: When a merchant arrives, the destination village's wealth increases (they sold goods), and the origin's decreases (they bought goods). For abstract villages, just adjust the Wealth stat.
   - For Millbrook (full sim): the merchant actually buys from shops (use the existing shop system).
6. **Deterministic**: Merchant decisions use seeded RNG (which route, how much to buy).

## Tests required
- `GoodsFlowAlongRoutes`: Merchant buys apples in Millbrook, travels, sells in King's Rest; verify wealth changes.
- `PricesDifferByVillage`: Verify King's Rest apple price > Millbrook apple price.
- `MerchantProfit`: Verify merchant makes a profit (or loss if prices are bad).
- `TradeTakesTime`: Merchant departs day 1, arrives day 3 (2-day route); goods not available until arrival.
