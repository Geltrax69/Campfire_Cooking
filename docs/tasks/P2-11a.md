# P2-11a: Daily-life drivers (village assembly, eating, shopping, meetings)

**Role:** Agents + Knowledge (assembly is test-side)
**Branch:** task/P2-11a-daily-life
**Depends on:** P2-01 … P2-10

## Goal
The village comes alive: one assembly wires every Phase 2 system in order,
and NPCs eat, buy food, and meet at the tavern in the evening — so the
acceptance simulation (P2-11b) has real daily life to observe.

## Context
All Phase 2 systems exist but nothing drives NPCs through a day: the
harness-only `BuyerActionSystem`/`MeetingSystem` in AppleTestHarness.cs are
scripted, and P2-03 left `RumorTrustFilter.ForConversation`,
`ConversationChoice.ChoosePartner` and `FriendPricing.ForShopkeeper` with no
runtime callers. This task builds the real drivers as Runtime systems plus
the full village assembly the acceptance sim will run.

## Requirements
1. **Village assembly** (`SimulationTests/Tools/VillageAssembly.cs` or
   similar): builds a full `WorldState` from approved Content — NPC
   definitions, all Phase 2 setups in dependency order (document the order;
   known constraints: MoneySourcesSetup before DebtSetup, setups before
   system registration), all systems registered with the right phases.
   NPCs start with 2 days of food in their inventories OR buy on day 1 —
   your choice, document it (farm households grow much of their own food;
   keep the rule simple and uniform).
2. **Eating** (`Agents/Systems/`): hungry NPCs eat food from their own
   inventory; hunger drops by the food's effect, using
   `Consumption.EffectiveEffects` (P2-09's hook) so stale food applies its
   stale effect. Deterministic; emits a quiet event.
3. **Shopping** (`Agents/Systems/`): NPCs whose food stock is low and who
   have copper buy from the village shops (bakery for bread, general store
   for the rest) via `Shop.Purchase`. Buys a simple rule-based amount
   (e.g. 2 days' worth — document it). Uses the NPC's own knowledge of
   shop locations (public village knowledge, not world truth).
4. **Evening meetings** (Knowledge or Agents — your choice, document it):
   during evening hours, NPCs whose schedule has them at the tavern
   converse: pick partners with `ConversationChoice.ChoosePartner`, share
   rumors via `RumorExchange.Share` with `RumorTrustFilter.ForConversation`.
   Deterministic order, seeded RNG.
5. **Friend pricing wiring**: the assembly sets each shop's
   `DiscountPolicy = FriendPricing.ForShopkeeper(state, owner)` so
   Bessa→Ralf etc. work in the live village.

## Tests required
- Assembly builds the full village without throwing; all expected systems
  registered; all 20 NPCs present with starting food.
- A hungry NPC eats: hunger drops, stale food applies the stale effect.
- A low-food NPC with copper buys bread: shop stock drops, wallet drops,
  purchase event logged.
- A tavern evening produces conversations and rumor spreads (trusted
  speaker's rumor adopted).
- Friend pricing active in the assembled village (Ralf pays less than a
  stranger at Bessa's).
- A 7-day village run: no exceptions, money conserved, NPCs alive and fed.
- Full suite green, Debug + Release.
