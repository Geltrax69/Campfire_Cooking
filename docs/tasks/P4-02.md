# P4-02: Ecosystem dynamics

**Role:** Agents + Economy agent
**Branch:** task/P4-02-ecosystem
**Depends on:** P4-01

## Goal
Implement ecosystem dynamics: predation, breeding, winter pressure, and livestock losses. Populations push on each other and on the village economy with no scripted triggers.

## Context
Phase 4 is "Animal ecosystem and taming". P4-01 built animal state. This task adds the systems that make populations live: wolves hunt deer and livestock, deer breed in spring, hard winters push deer to farms and wolves to livestock, and the village loses 2-4 livestock per winter.

## Requirements
1. **PredationSystem** (IWorldSystem):
   - Wolves hunt: each wolf has a daily chance to kill a deer (higher in winter). Use seeded RNG.
   - Wolves take livestock when desperate (winter, low deer): 2-4 livestock incidents per winter (per WORLD.md, load-bearing).
   - When a kill happens: remove the prey animal, record a truth event (Predation), add `item_pelt` to the hunter's inventory (if a villager) or just remove (if wolves).
   - Boars are never prey (too dangerous); bramblebacks are prey for foxes (flavor, not simulated in detail).
2. **BreedingSystem** (IWorldSystem):
   - Spring: chickens hatch chicks (hens hatch 6-10 if allowed), pigs farrow (~7 per sow), deer drop fawns (1 per doe), wolves have pups (3-4), bramblebacks have young (2-3 per pair).
   - New animals are created with age=young, trust=0 (wild) or 20 (domestic).
   - Cap populations at design maxima (e.g., deer ~40, wolves 5-7) — excess young are "eaten or traded" (removed with a truth event).
3. **WinterPressureSystem** (IWorldSystem):
   - In winter: deer range to farm fields (location changes to `loc_farm`), wolves range to `loc_forest_edge`.
   - Livestock losses: 2-4 per winter, distributed across the season (not all at once).
   - Crop damage: deer at farms cause a small economic loss (record as truth; P5 can use it for town development).
4. **Bounty**: Wolf pelts are worth 10 copper base; the crown's 50-copper winter bounty makes wolf hunting pay.
   - When a villager kills a wolf (via hunting, not yet implemented — just the pelt value), they get 10 copper (or 50 in winter with bounty).
   - Document: actual wolf hunting by villagers is future work (needs the hunting system).
5. **Egg production**: Chickens lay ~0.7 eggs/day in warm months, ~0.2 in winter.
   - Add eggs to the farm's inventory daily (or to a coop inventory).
   - This feeds the economy (Maren's egg sales).
6. **Deterministic**: All randomness uses the world's seeded RNG. Seasonal timing uses the game clock.

## Tests required
- `WinterIncreasesLivestockLosses`: Simulate a winter; verify 2-4 livestock are lost.
- `WolfPredationReducesDeer`: Wolves hunt deer; deer population decreases over a month.
- `SpringBreedingIncreasesPopulations`: Spring breeding adds young animals; populations grow.
- `EggProductionSeasonal`: Chickens lay more eggs in summer than winter.
- `PopulationCapsEnforced`: Deer population does not exceed ~40; excess young are removed.
