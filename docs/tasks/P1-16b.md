# P1-16b: Production, restock orders and deliveries

**Role:** Economy
**Branch:** task/P1-16b-restocking
**Depends on:** P1-07, P1-10, P1-16a

## Goal
Add general, caller-configured item production and atomic wholesale restocking so
low shop stock can trigger a real order fulfilled from a producer's inventory.

## Context
The Apple Test stall must never reset. Apples enter only through production/delivery,
and wholesale copper moves from the shopkeeper to the producer.

## You may edit
- `Packages/com.geltrax.livingworld.simulation/Runtime/Economy/`
- `SimulationTests/Economy/`

## You must not edit
- Anything outside the folders above. Use the P1-16a Core event vocabulary as-is.

## Requirements
1. Add validated, immutable caller-supplied production and restock configurations; no hard-coded apple/farm values.
2. Production adds the configured quantity at the configured eligible time exactly once and records `Produced` truth.
3. Stock below an explicit threshold creates at most one pending order and records `RestockOrdered` truth.
4. Fulfillment transfers the available configured quantity atomically from producer to shop, transfers exact integer
   wholesale copper in the opposite direction, and records `Restocked` truth.
5. Insufficient stock or copper must not partially mutate unless partial fulfillment was explicitly configured.
6. Items and copper are conserved across delivery; shop stock never resets.
7. Process configs/orders in stable ordinal order and remain deterministic, C# 9 compatible, and Unity-free.

## Tests required
- Production happens once at its configured time and not on repeated ticks.
- Stock below 10 can trigger one order; adequate stock triggers none.
- A 45-item delivery at 1 copper transfers 45 items/45 copper and conserves totals.
- Insufficient producer stock/shop copper follows explicit full-or-partial policy atomically.
- Repeated ticks do not duplicate production, orders or delivery.
